FAQ

Questions people ask before they search

Is Tee-Time free?

Yes. Searching live tee times and opening the course booking page are free. Alerts use Google sign-in. We do not add a booking markup.

Do I book on Tee-Time or the golf course website?

You book on the course’s own tee sheet. We find open times across Arizona, Utah, and Idaho and send you to the course site.

Which states have live tee times?

Arizona, Utah, and Idaho are live. Colorado and Nevada are next. See tee times by city.

Do I need an account to search?

No. Search and compare without an account. Sign in with Google to create alerts or share a vote link with your group.
